Privacy Policy

PetLibro Timer · Operated by Chris Koch · Effective October 18, 2026

PetLibro Timer is a private Android app used by a single household to control a PetLibro wet-food feeder and check its food dish with a Google Nest camera. It is not distributed to the public.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Nest camera (Smart Device Management API). With the account owner's consent, the app opens a short live stream to the Nest camera aimed at the feeder and captures a single still frame of the food dish when a food check runs.
  • PetLibro account. The app signs in to the owner's PetLibro account to open, close, and rotate the feeder.

How the data is used

  • The captured frame is sent to Google's Gemini API solely to classify whether the dish has food, is empty, or the lid is closed.
  • The result and the frame are shown on the owner's phone and in a notification.
  • No data is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than the food check.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens, settings, and captured frames are kept only on the owner's phone.
  • There is no developer server. No data is collected by, sold to, or shared with anyone, other than the Google and PetLibro services needed to run the features above.

Google API Services

PetLibro Timer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access to the Nest camera can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the phone.
