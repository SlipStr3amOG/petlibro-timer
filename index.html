PetLibro Timer

A personal Android app by Chris Koch.

What it does

  • Opens and closes a PetLibro Polar wet-food feeder on a timer.
  • Uses a Google Nest camera aimed at the feeder to check whether the food dish is empty, and sends a notification if it is.

Who it's for

This is a private, single-household app. It is not offered on any app store or to the public.
